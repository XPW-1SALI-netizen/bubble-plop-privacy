Privacy Policy – Bubble Plop

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bubble Plop handles information when you use the app.

Privacy at a glance: Bubble Plop does not require a user account. The current version does not use the developer's own server to store personal player data. Game settings and progress may be stored locally on your device. Optional in-app purchases are processed through Google Play.

1. Developer and privacy contact

Wis-Ali Bagaschew

Email: bubbleplop.support@gmail.com

2. Information required to play

Bubble Plop does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, postal address, telephone number, contacts, photos, microphone data, camera data, or precise location in order to play.

3. Locally stored game data

The app may store game-related information locally on your device, such as settings, high scores, game progress, and locally unlocked content. This information is used to provide the game's functionality and is not uploaded to a proprietary server operated by the developer.

4. In-app purchases and Google Play Billing

Bubble Plop offers optional in-app purchases, including virtual in-game content or currency such as Bubbles. Purchases are processed using the Google Play billing system.

The developer does not directly collect or store your full payment card or bank account details. Payment processing is handled by Google.

To process, verify, unlock, or restore a purchase, the app may exchange technical purchase information with Google Play, such as a product identifier, purchase status, and purchase token where provided by the Google Play Billing interface. In the current version, this information is not sent to a proprietary backend server operated by the developer.

5. Restore Purchases

If the app provides a “Restore Purchases” function, it may query Google Play to determine whether eligible purchases are associated with the Google account currently used on the device. This allows previously purchased content to be restored. The developer does not receive your complete payment details.

6. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of Bubble Plop does not intentionally include third-party advertising SDKs, personalized advertising, or independently integrated analytics or tracking services.

If advertising, analytics, cloud services, or other third-party SDKs are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the corresponding Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

7. App permissions

Bubble Plop does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location for its core gameplay. If a future version requires additional permissions, the relevant purpose and data handling will be disclosed.

8. Sharing of personal data

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share personal player data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Google Play is used as an external service for in-app purchases. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own applicable privacy terms and policies.

9. Support communications

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, the information you provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message, will be used to respond to and manage your request.

Such correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain necessary records, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Legal basis for support communications

Where the GDPR applies, support correspondence is processed as necessary to respond to your request and, where applicable, on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in providing user support, maintaining the app, preventing abuse, and documenting relevant communications.

11. Data retention and deletion

Because the current version does not create user accounts or store personal player profiles on a proprietary developer server, there is generally no server-side player profile held by the developer that needs to be deleted.

Locally stored app data can generally be removed by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Records held by Google in connection with Google Play purchases are subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

12. Data security

The developer aims to limit data processing to what is necessary for the app's operation and support. Payment information is not stored on a proprietary developer server, and payment processing relies on the security mechanisms provided by Google Play.

13. Children

Bubble Plop does not require users to provide personal contact information in order to play. The current version does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or other direct contact information from children through the gameplay itself.

Where special legal or platform requirements apply to younger users, the app will be configured and maintained in accordance with the applicable requirements.

14. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under data protection law, including the GDPR, you may have rights concerning personal data processed by the developer, including the rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or to object to certain processing.

To make a privacy request, contact bubbleplop.support@gmail.com. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

15. International availability

This Privacy Policy is publicly available regardless of where the app is downloaded. Third-party services such as Google Play may process information in accordance with their own global infrastructure and privacy policies.

16. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this public URL, and the “Last updated” date above will be revised when material changes are made.
